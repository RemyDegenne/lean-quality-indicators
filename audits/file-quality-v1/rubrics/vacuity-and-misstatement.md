# Vacuous or misleading results

Look for results that fail to express their documented mathematical purpose because of
inconsistent assumptions, an unintentionally empty domain, a definition that trivialises
the claim, or a missing condition or conclusion. Read the definitions and surrounding
documentation. Give an explicit derivation of the vacuity or a concrete example separating
the actual statement from the intended one, and propose a repair.

An intentionally empty case, a legitimate convention for junk values, a tautological
helper lemma or an elementary theorem is not automatically a defect. Require evidence
of the intended purpose and the specific mismatch; do not speculate about an unstated aim.
