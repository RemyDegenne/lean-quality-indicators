# Proof simplification without loss of readability

Find a proof that can be simplified or golfed without making it harder for a reader to
interpret. Quote the existing proof and give a concrete replacement Lean term or tactic
block. Explain both the simplification and why the reader can still follow the key
mathematical idea at least as easily. Read every proposed replacement lemma in the sources.

Removing redundant intermediate steps, using a well-named lemma or avoiding unnecessary
case splits can count. Fewer characters alone do not count. Do not replace a comprehensible
argument with opaque automation, obscure syntax, implicit inference tricks or a clever
term that takes more effort to understand. If the improvement is merely personal taste,
or readability is uncertain, do not flag it. Do not claim the replacement was compiled.
